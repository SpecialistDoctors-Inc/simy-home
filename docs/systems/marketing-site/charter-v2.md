## Source-grounded market and URL boundary

The previously observed Ahrefs market set is Japan, US, UK, India, Spain, Mexico, France and Singapore; the current site languages are English, Japanese, Hindi, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese. Prior observations under `docs/seo/ahrefs-observations-2026-10-03.json` concern general AI-agent/workflow terms, not validated demand for each new sales/engineering/tool URL. Research priority for this redo: the eight markets' current general terms; Japan and English-market sales/workflow/CLI intents for the new JA/EN pages; only extend to Spanish/French/Hindi/Chinese new pages when query evidence and authentic localized content justify it. Preserve country and language as different dimensions. If Ahrefs quota blocks fresh evidence, retain the dated 2026-10-03 observations with their limits and mark new page/market combinations unvalidated; do not infer volume from broad seed terms.

Proposed route map to validate before implementation: `/for/` and `/for/en/` answer occupation choice; `/for/sales/`, `/for/en/sales/` answer sales applicability; existing `/for/engineers/`, `/for/en/engineers/` answer engineering applicability. `/guides/{topic}.html` and `/guides/en/{topic}.html` answer one practical setup/workflow intent each for getting started, SIMY CLI Delivery Loop, SQM, Conductor.build, Claude Code. Existing six-language home, guide and download URLs retain their current language promises. Do not redirect or create country clones without distinct verified needs. Related occupation and guide pages cross-link within the visitor's language and lead to a real next step. An English fallback from other languages is labelled. Existing route behavior and S3 literal slash-key publishing require actual inspection; this is a proposed To-Be, not evidence it works.

For UX-02/03 first-read assessment, give a reviewer who did not author the page a neutral job scenario and the rendered page without coaching. The release decision is based on current technical and experience guardrails, not an arbitrary required ranking uplift. Owner of post-release observation is the site owner (user); no recurring automation is implied. Roll back if a released core path fails or sends a visitor to the wrong language/topic; hold if the exact candidate lacks applicable independent or CI/SQM evidence.
