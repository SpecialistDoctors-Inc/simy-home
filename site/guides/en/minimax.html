01 / REFERENCE

What is MiniMax? H3, M3, pricing, API and running it locally (in depth)

Open these when you want details on MiniMax itself. Model names and prices are as of October 2026.

What is MiniMax, and where is it from?An AI company in Shanghai, China, with text, video and speech models

Who makes MiniMax

MiniMax is an AI company based in Shanghai, China. Alongside large language models (LLMs) for text, it builds its own video generation and speech synthesis models and apps. Its international apps are provided by Nanonoble Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore.

Three ways to use it

  • Apps: MiniMax Code, MiniMax Audio, Hailuo for video and more, on the web or your phone.
  • API: build it into your own systems or developer tools through MiniMax's platform (platform.minimax.io). Pay-as-you-go and a flat-rate Token Plan are available.
  • Open weights: run the MiniMax-M3 or MiniMax H3 weights published on Hugging Face on your own servers, subject to license terms.

"Minimax" is also the name of a search algorithm in game theory. This page is about MiniMax the AI company.

What is MiniMax H3?

MiniMax H3 is a video generation model MiniMax announced in late July 2026. It takes text, images, video and audio as cues and produces video with stereo sound (4 to 15 seconds, up to 2K). The official model card says it supports 11 languages for spoken dialogue, including Japanese.

  • Where to use it: the Hailuo video app, MiniMax's API, and the weights published on Hugging Face.
  • How it differs from M3: H3 makes video; M3 is a text model. They are different models.
  • Running it locally: it's heavy for a personal computer. See the reality of running it locally below.

What is MiniMax-M3?

MiniMax-M3 is MiniMax's current flagship text model, released in June 2026. It has about 428 billion parameters in total (about 23 billion active per inference), a 1M-token context, and accepts text, image and video input. API prices are low, and there's a Token Plan for flat-rate use from coding tools.

How to use MiniMax Audio

Paste the text you want read aloud, choose a voice and speaking speed, and generate the audio. It works for training video narration, product voiceovers and internal announcements. If you make a voice that resembles a real person, get their consent and check the rules of wherever you'll publish it.

What MiniMax can do at workText, voice and video from one company's tools
  • Text: draft scripts, articles, emails and reports with MiniMax-M3. It can also summarize long documents and explain images or video you show it.
  • Voice: turn scripts into narration and voiceovers with MiniMax Audio, for training, product introductions and internal announcements.
  • Video: use MiniMax H3 in Hailuo or via the API to make short videos with sound from text or images. Good for storyboard-style drafts and test clips for social media.
  • Coding: hand off coding work to MiniMax Code, or to coding tools connected through the Anthropic-compatible API.

Making a video in Hailuo (H3)

  1. Describe the scene in words. Be specific about place, people and action, such as "a morning office where staff hold a meeting while looking at a tablet".
  2. Add an image if needed. You can animate a product photo or a reference image.
  3. Make several and choose. The same prompt gives different results each time, so comparing a few candidates is the quickest path.

If you use faces, logos or anything resembling someone else's work, check the rights and the rules of where you'll publish. Have a person review any video before it goes outside the company.

Whatever the use, MiniMax delivers assets and drafts. The owners and deadlines decided in meetings, and the messages to the people involved, are what SIMY picks up from meetings and chat and moves forward.

How pricing works: pay-as-you-go and the Token PlanText API is on the cheap side. Flat rate starts at $22 a month
  • Pay-as-you-go: as of October 2026, MiniMax-M3 costs $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens for inputs up to 512K tokens. Longer inputs cost twice as much, and priority processing costs 1.5 times as much. That's on the cheap side even among Chinese LLMs.
  • Token Plan: a flat-rate plan for use from coding tools and similar, in three tiers: Plus at $22 a month, Max at $55 and Ultra at $132. Each has usage limits per five hours and per week.
  • Taxes: prices are in US dollars. Check the pay-as-you-go pricing page and the Token Plan page for tax treatment and the latest figures.

Which to choose

  • You use it daily from coding tools: if your usage is hard to predict, the Token Plan makes the monthly bill easier to plan.
  • You're building it into your own systems: pay-as-you-go, which scales with volume, fits better. Start small, check the cost per task, then expand.

Video and audio are priced separately from text and vary with the number and length of what you make, so check the official pages.

The MiniMax API: Anthropic-compatible is recommendedConnect it to Claude Code and other tools that speak the Anthropic format
  • Getting in: create an API key on MiniMax's platform (platform.minimax.io).
  • Compatibility: MiniMax recommends a format compatible with Anthropic's SDK. It also supports OpenAI's Chat Completions and Responses API formats.
  • From coding tools: point Claude Code or another Anthropic-format tool at MiniMax's endpoint and API key, and you can call MiniMax-M3. The official docs walk through it.

Getting started with the API

  1. Create an account on MiniMax's platform. Choose pay-as-you-go or the Token Plan.
  2. Create an API key. Keep it in an environment variable, never directly in code or shared documents.
  3. Point your usual tools at it. Pick the Anthropic or OpenAI format, then swap in the URL and model name.
  4. Start small and watch usage. Long inputs cost more per token. Check daily usage before scaling up to production volume.

How SIMY relates: SIMY doesn't connect MiniMax API keys at this time. SIMY's cloud runs use your ChatGPT (Codex) account.

Open weights and licensing: commercial use has conditionsM3 and H3 both use custom licenses. Don't lump them in as "open source"

The MiniMax-M3 weights are published on Hugging Face, but under MiniMax's own "MiniMax Community License". Non-commercial use is free; commercial use comes with conditions.

  • Commercial use: you must display "Built with MiniMax M3" and notify MiniMax.
  • Annual revenue above $20 million: you need MiniMax's written permission before commercial use.
  • Prohibited uses: military use and others are prohibited.
  • M2.7: also under its own license.

The MiniMax H3 license

  • Territory: the MiniMax H3 Community License excludes the EU, the UK, South Korea and the US from its territory. If you operate in or work with partners in those regions, check carefully before using it.
  • Commercial use: you must show "MiniMax H3" in your product's interface. Companies with annual revenue above $20 million need written permission first.

Even for internal trials, the terms depend on whether the use counts as commercial. Before using it for work, read the M3 license text and the H3 license text.

Can you run MiniMax H3 or M3 locally?Both are heavy for a personal computer. Official guides assume multiple GPUs

Lots of people search for "minimax h3 local", but as of October 2026, expect it to be hard to run H3 or M3 on an ordinary computer.

  • Ollama: minimax-m3 and minimax-m2.7 in the official library carry the "cloud" tag. That means they run in Ollama's cloud, and your input is sent to Ollama's servers. Community-built versions exist, but they aren't official.
  • MiniMax H3: the weights are public, but the official instructions use a four-GPU setup as the example. There's also a guide for using it in ComfyUI; check the GPU memory it needs on your own hardware.
  • Official guides for M3 and M2.7: M3 is shown on eight B200 GPUs (marked experimental), and M2.7 on four high-memory GPUs. These are data-center setups.
  • If you want something on your own computer: the small text models covered in the Qwen guide and the GLM guide are options.

If the goal is keeping data in-house, cloud-tagged models don't qualify. Either run the weights on your own GPU servers or choose a model small enough to run locally.

Languages: how well does MiniMax work in yours?You can ask in many languages, but M3's supported languages aren't officially listed

You can prompt MiniMax-M3 in English and many other languages, but as of October 2026, M3's supported languages aren't officially listed. The H3 video model's card, on the other hand, lists 11 languages for spoken dialogue. Either way, test with real material from your own work.

  • Text: check the tone and phrasing of scripts and email drafts before using them.
  • Voice: listen for how names, product terms and numbers are read aloud.
  • Video: check that any on-screen text isn't garbled. Adding subtitles afterwards in an editor is the safe route.

You can also chain it: translate a script into another language with M3, then voice it with MiniMax Audio. The owners and deadlines for the fixes agreed in the meeting, and the messages to the production company and your team, can be left to SIMY.

09 / FAQ

FAQ

What is MiniMax?

MiniMax is an AI company based in Shanghai, China. It builds models and apps including MiniMax H3 for video generation, MiniMax-M3 for text, and MiniMax Audio for speech synthesis.

Which country is MiniMax from?

It is developed by a company in Shanghai, China. Its international apps are provided by Nanonoble Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore.

What is MiniMax H3?

MiniMax H3 is a generative model for video with sound, announced by MiniMax in late July 2026. It creates videos up to 15 seconds long from text, images, video and audio, and is available in Hailuo and through the API. It is a separate model from MiniMax-M3, which handles text.

Can I run MiniMax H3 locally?

Expect it to be hard on a personal computer. The weights are published on Hugging Face, but the official instructions use a four-GPU setup as the example. To try it easily, Hailuo or the API is the realistic route.

Is MiniMax free to use?

It varies by app, so check each app's official information for what's free. The API is paid, either pay-as-you-go or a monthly Token Plan.

How much does the MiniMax Token Plan cost?

As of October 2026 there are three tiers: Plus at $22 a month, Max at $55 and Ultra at $132. Each has usage limits per five hours and per week.

What is MiniMax Audio?

MiniMax Audio is MiniMax's speech synthesis app and model, turning text into natural-sounding speech. It works well for training video narration or product voiceovers.

Can I use MiniMax models from Claude Code?

Yes. MiniMax recommends an Anthropic-compatible API format, so you can call MiniMax-M3 from Claude Code and similar tools by swapping in MiniMax's endpoint and API key.

Can I run MiniMax on my own computer?

Running the current MiniMax-M3 on a personal computer isn't realistic. In Ollama's official library, M3 carries the cloud tag (it runs in Ollama's cloud), and the official self-hosting guide assumes several high-end GPUs.

Can MiniMax models be used commercially?

Yes, with conditions. Commercial use of MiniMax-M3 requires a "Built with MiniMax M3" notice and notifying MiniMax, and companies with annual revenue above $20 million need written permission first. The MiniMax H3 license excludes the EU, the UK, South Korea and the US from its territory, so check where you'll use it.

Where is the data I enter into MiniMax stored?

The policy for the international apps says data is, in principle, not transferred outside Singapore. The API has a separate policy, so read it before you sign up.

Does SIMY run on MiniMax?

No. SIMY doesn't connect to MiniMax at this time; it runs on your ChatGPT (Codex) account and Claude Code on your computer. You can bring scripts and assets made with MiniMax straight into your meetings and chats.

Do I have to ask SIMY every time for the work after MiniMax?

No. SIMY picks up decisions, owners and deadlines from meeting records and chats on its own. It learns your way of working from conversations and returns only the points that need your decision.
