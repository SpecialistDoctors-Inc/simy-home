PROGRAM TERMS

Understand the terms
before sharing.

SIMY terms informed by the Japanese PLAUD affiliate program. Eligible products are monthly or annual Starter and SIMY by Industry. Payments and commissions are in USD. Legacy plans and add-ons are excluded. This does not indicate a partnership with PLAUD.

10% of the first eligible payment.

Calculated on the discounted, tax-exclusive USD cash payment for monthly or annual Starter or SIMY by Industry. The first paid invoice after a free trial is included. Renewals are excluded.

A 14-day attribution window.

The last eligible referral before authenticated checkout is fixed within a 14-day window and retained through the trial and payment retries. Commission is reviewed for 50 days after payment. Any partial or full refund cancels the entire commission for that invoice.

Request payout from $100 USD.

Request a payout after the eligible balance reaches $100 USD. Payments use a monthly cutoff and are due by the end of the following month, in USD to a verified recipient. SIMY covers sender transfer fees; recipient bank fees are the affiliate’s responsibility. No automatic currency conversion. Applications, tracking and transfers have not started.

Reference: PLAUD Japan affiliate program (Japanese). Clicks alone do not earn commissions.

Understand the earnings and payout threshold.

One Starter monthly subscription at $35.80 before tax earns $3.58 on its first eligible payment. About 28 such confirmed referrals are needed to reach the $100 payout threshold. A Starter annual payment of $357.60 before tax earns $35.76. Discounts, refunds and eligibility affect actual earnings. Clicks alone do not earn commissions.

After launch, approved affiliates can copy locale-specific links, advertising-disclosed HTML and banners from their dashboard. Payout recipients are verified through a protected process; never put bank details on your blog or this public page.
