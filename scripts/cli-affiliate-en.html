PROGRAM PROPOSAL

Understand the terms
before sharing.

A SIMY proposal informed by the Japanese PLAUD affiliate program. Eligible products, currency and final conditions will be confirmed before launch. This does not indicate a partnership with PLAUD.

10% of the first eligible payment.

Calculated on the discounted, tax-exclusive cash payment for an eligible plan. The first paid invoice after a free trial is included. Renewals are excluded.

A 14-day attribution window.

Attribution is fixed at an eligible authenticated checkout within 14 days of the referral. Commission is reviewed for 50 days after payment. Any partial or full refund cancels the entire commission for that invoice.

Request payout from JPY10,000.

Request a payout after the eligible balance reaches JPY10,000. The proposal uses a monthly cutoff, with payment by the end of the following month. Transfers are not connected or active.

Reference: PLAUD Japan affiliate program (Japanese). Clicks alone do not earn commissions.
